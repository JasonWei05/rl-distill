Chess RL grid: 20M, 50M, 100M from matched SFT bases

Three released Qwen3 pretraining checkpoints, each SFT'd for 384 steps on search-trace CoT (their 3-epoch recipe, YaRN to 3,072 tokens). Pretraining budgets are chosen so all three bases land near the 20M's benchmark pass@1. Each base then runs GRPO with four group-size × batch configs, at lr 5e-5 and again at 2.5e-5.
